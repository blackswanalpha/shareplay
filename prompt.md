# Landing Background Image Prompt

## For: `public/images/landing-bg.webp`

### Prompt

A cinematic wide-angle photograph of a dark home theater room at night. Two silhouetted figures sit on a modern couch facing a massive glowing screen, bathed in deep red and crimson ambient light. The screen casts soft volumetric light rays into the hazy air. Subtle red LED strips line the ceiling edges and behind the screen. The room fades into pure black at the corners and edges. Moody, atmospheric, shallow depth of field with the figures slightly out of focus. Ultra-wide 16:9 composition. No text, no UI, no logos.

### Style Keywords

Cinematic, dark key lighting, volumetric light, red color grading, atmospheric haze, shallow DOF, film grain, anamorphic lens, moody, editorial photography, ultra-realistic

### Negative Prompt

Bright, colorful, daylight, cartoon, illustration, text, watermark, logo, cluttered, busy, overexposed, neon, blue light, green light

### Specs

- **Aspect ratio**: 16:9 (landscape)
- **Resolution**: 1920x1080 minimum, 2560x1440 preferred
- **Format**: Export as `.webp` (quality 85)
- **Placement**: Save to `public/images/landing-bg.webp`
- **Color palette**: Black (#000), deep red (#ff3b3b), dark crimson (#661717), charcoal (#111)
- **Key requirement**: Edges must fade to near-pure black so the vignette overlay blends seamlessly
