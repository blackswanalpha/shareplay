# Ambient Audio Prompt

## For: `public/audio/ambient.mp3`

### Description

A deep, cinematic ambient drone loop for a dark-themed media sharing app landing page. The sound should feel like sitting in a premium home theater moments before the movie starts — intimate, immersive, and slightly suspenseful.

### Prompt

Dark cinematic ambient drone. Deep sub-bass hum with slow, evolving warm pad textures. Subtle analog tape hiss underneath. Occasional soft, distant reverb swells that rise and fade like breath. A faint low-frequency pulse every 8-10 seconds, felt more than heard. No melody, no rhythm, no beats. Think: the sound of an empty cinema after the lights dim but before the film rolls. Seamless loop with no audible cut point.

### Mood & References

- The lobby ambience from a David Fincher film
- Blade Runner 2049 interiors (quiet scenes)
- The hum of a high-end sound system at idle
- A server room at 3am — warm, constant, alive

### Technical Specs

- **Duration**: 30-60 seconds (seamless loop)
- **Format**: MP3, 192kbps or higher
- **Sample rate**: 44.1kHz
- **Channels**: Stereo
- **Loudness**: -24 LUFS (quiet, non-intrusive)
- **Fade**: Built-in 2s crossfade at loop point for seamless looping
- **Placement**: Save to `public/audio/ambient.mp3`

### Key Constraints

- Must loop seamlessly — no clicks, pops, or audible seam
- Volume should sit well below speech level; this is background texture, not foreground audio
- No musical elements (no melody, chords, or beat)
- No nature sounds (no rain, wind, birds)
- Warm, not cold — analog character, not digital sterility
